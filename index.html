How it works

From sighting
to resolved

The entire process from spotting a hazardous animal to campus security being notified takes under 60 seconds.

1

Sign in with your M365 account

Click "Report a sighting" and log in with your Belgium Campus Microsoft 365 student credentials. No separate account needed.

2

Fill in the report form

Select the animal type, severity level, your location on campus, and optionally attach a photo. The form takes under a minute to complete.

3

Automatic alert fires

If the severity is high, Power Automate immediately emails the campus security team with the full report details and location.

4

Security responds

Campus admin monitors all incidents on the live Power BI dashboard. Your report is logged, tracked, and resolved.
